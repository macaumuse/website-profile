Regional profile display:
- AU visitors see the Australian appointments and UQ email, without the Zhejiang University appointment, professor heading or email.
- CN, HK and MO visitors retain the Zhejiang University profile and email and do not load the Australian appointments or UQ email.
- All other recognised regions retain both sets of appointments and both email addresses.
- While geolocation is pending or unavailable, country-dependent appointments and email addresses stay hidden; the page heading uses a neutral academic-profile label.
- Shared page metadata and portrait alternative text do not advertise a country-specific appointment.

Country detection uses Cloudflare's same-origin `/cdn-cgi/trace`. These are presentation rules for a public static website, not access controls on its files.
